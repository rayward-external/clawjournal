How would you like to contribute?

To separately share full original and redacted conversations only to improve the redaction pipeline, use clawjournal redaction-eval --store YOUR_STORE pairs settings. The snippet permission below never includes originals.

Review each contribution is the default. You can inspect and approve each small snippet separately.

Allow ongoing contributions lets the local batch command prepare eligible examples without asking for permission each time. This covers only conversations captured into this local evaluation store after you enable it, for the ClawJournal project's redaction-model training and evaluation.

Labelled personal identifiers are replaced with invented values. The surrounding text is included, and automated checks can miss private information. Uncertain or unsupported matches are held for review. Automatic labels stay drafts; ongoing consent does not certify that the labels or surrounding text were checked by a person.

This preference does not upload anything or change ordinary ClawJournal upload permissions. The batch command writes local bundles. Delivering them is a separate action. Secret scanning may contact credential providers during the final verification step.



Turn off ongoing contributions


You can stop at any time. This also removes managed local automatic bundles. Files already copied or delivered elsewhere cannot be recalled by this command. Earlier individual approvals stay separate.
